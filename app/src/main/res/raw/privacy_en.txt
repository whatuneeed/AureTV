Privacy

The launcher needs no account and collects no personal data. Below is what stays on the device and what goes over the network.

Stored on the device

Tile order, folders, hidden apps, screen saver settings and the chosen language are kept locally in app storage. They never leave the TV and are removed with the app.

Not collected

The launcher sends no analytics, crash reports, device identifiers, installed-app lists or launch history. It contains no advertising or analytics libraries.

Network requests

The only external host is Apple's public server, used to download the aerial catalogue, posters and video clips. Those requests carry no personal data beyond what HTTPS needs. Aerial caches stay on the device and can be cleared in Storage.

Permissions

Internet access is used by the screen saver. Network state access is used only to show the connection type and IP address on the Network page. The launcher does not request location, contacts, microphone or user storage access.

Third-party apps

Apps you start from the home screen handle data under their own policies. The launcher does not see their content and does not pass information about you to them.

Questions

The launcher is an open project: its network code and permission list can be reviewed in the sources.
